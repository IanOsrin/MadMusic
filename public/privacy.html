Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026 · Music Africa Direct ("MAD Music", "we")

MAD Music is a South African music streaming service operated at musicafricadirect.com, on our mobile site, and through the MAD Music app. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We comply with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and the access pass (token) linked to it, so we can sign you in, keep your playlists and library, and apply your pass's device limit.
  • Listening activity. Which tracks you play and for how long. We use this to run the service, calculate royalty reporting for artists and rights holders, and improve what we recommend.
  • Technical logs. IP address, device/browser type and session identifiers, collected automatically when you use the service — used for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.
  • Aggregate analytics. We use cookie-free analytics (Umami) that count visits and page views without tracking you across the web and without advertising identifiers.

Payments

Purchases on our website are processed by Paystack; purchases made through an app store are processed by that store (for example Google Play). Your card number is handled by the payment processor and never reaches or is stored on our servers. We keep only the confirmation that a payment succeeded and the pass it created.

How we use your information

  • To provide the service: sign-in, streaming, playlists, passes.
  • To pay the music's makers: aggregated play counts go into royalty reporting for artists, labels and rights holders. They receive totals, not your identity.
  • To protect the service: enforcing device limits and preventing abuse.
  • To fix and improve things: diagnosing errors and understanding which features are used.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not show third-party advertising or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other websites or apps.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers that make MAD Music run: our hosting provider, our content-delivery and storage providers, and our payment processors — each receiving only what they need to perform their function. Rights holders receive aggregated listening reports that do not identify you.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account information while your pass is active and for a reasonable period afterwards so you can return without losing your playlists. You can ask us to delete your account and associated personal information at any time by emailing the address below; we will action deletion requests within 30 days.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the address below and we'll respond promptly. If you're not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Children

MAD Music is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

All traffic to MAD Music is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to personal information is restricted to the small team that operates the service.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be flagged in the app or on the site.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: info@musicafricadirect.com
